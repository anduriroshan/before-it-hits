- [x] **The plan can say "nothing" (14 Oct).** A product that hands a city a spending list every time it is opened teaches them to stop reading the list, and most days are ordinary. Two deterministic gates before any spending: is this an ordinary amount of rain — judged on the **India Meteorological Department's own published daily classes**, light rain being up to 15.5 mm, not on anything we invented — and if not, does the rain actually reach a spot worth acting on. Either way the answer is no measures, the reason, and the rainfall it would take to change it. Crucially it still says how many low spots take water anyway ("84 of the 194 … they take water on any wet day"), because the honest point is that water in a low spot is not on its own a reason to spend. The quiet verdict carries its own limits: it is about this box and this rainfall, the screening has no drain data so a quiet answer is the conservative one, and routine seasonal readiness is not in scope and is not cancelled by it. 7 tests
- [x] **Before / after (F10, 13 Oct).** The same box run twice: as it is, and with the spots the plan acts on taken out of the exposed set (`analyse_grid(treated=...)` for roads, `exposure.estimate(treated=...)` for people).
