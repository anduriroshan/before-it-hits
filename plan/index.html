Before It Hits — what we build, before we build it

Cities get a rainfall forecast and issue a warning for the whole city. The water still gathers in the same low spots it gathered in last year. This plan defines a tool where you draw a box on the map and ask what happens there — and get back which places the ground traps water in, why, who is nearby, and what this particular city can afford to do about it before the rain arrives.

Two tiers, and they explain each other

Honest resolution is the thing that shapes this whole product. A 30 m elevation model carries about half a metre of vertical error — the same size as the flood depths we would otherwise be quoting. So we split what we claim in two.
