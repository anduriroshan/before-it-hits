Maps per feature

What maps each feature needs, and how they’re processed

Almost everything now runs on demand, on the box you drew — that is the point of the selection tool. Only terrain staging happens once. The measured budget is roughly 2–4 seconds to read the elevation and well under a second for the physics, so the download is the slow part, not the hydrology.

Map types at a glance

Processing profiles — chosen from the box you drew

The feature contract never changes; the profile decides which method fills it, and it is picked automatically from where the box lands. Draw inside the coastal lowland and you get bare-earth terrain; draw inland past that coverage and you fall back to a surface model, flagged, because buildings then read as hills.
