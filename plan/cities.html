What the research changed

Chosen: Kochi

The only candidate with official depth-valued hazard rasters published by the state authority. That turns “trust our model” into “here is how our model compares to the authority’s own data”.

Rejected: Singapore

Easily the best APIs — open rainfall, flood alerts with named roads and coordinates. But no public national elevation data at all, and flood-prone land is down to about 23 hectares nationally. Almost nothing left to warn about.

Rejected: Vijayawada & Vizag

The strongest human story (35 deaths, ~270,000 affected in 2024). But AP’s GIS services are broken or login-gated, there is no official hazard raster, ward boundaries were redrawn in 2025, and Vijayawada likely sits outside the bare-earth elevation coverage.
